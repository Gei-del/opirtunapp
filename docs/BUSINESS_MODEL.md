# Business model

## Product thesis

OpirtunApp is not another job board. It is an application readiness
layer: one trusted evidence profile can be compared with many opportunities,
while the candidate remains the decision-maker.

## Primary customers

1. **Candidates** — students, career changers, caregivers, and early-career
   professionals who cannot spend hours interpreting every form.
2. **Universities and workforce organizations** — teams supporting many people
   with limited advising capacity.
3. **Opportunity providers** — foundations and employers that want complete,
   eligible applications without excluding people through complex forms.

## Revenue model

| Plan | Illustrative price | Value |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Candidate Free | $0 | Limited matching and one active draft |
| Candidate Pro | US$4.99/month | Unlimited drafts, reminders, evidence vault |
| Cohort | From US$299/month | Guided cohorts, aggregate completion analytics |
| Verified provider | From US$99/listing | Structured listing and applicant requirement checks |

Provider payment never changes ranking or eligibility. This separation protects
candidate trust and makes the matching explanation auditable.

## Go-to-market

- Start with university technology programs and scholarship communities in
  Colombia.
- Validate the readiness workflow through ten guided candidate interviews.
- Partner with one university employability office for a cohort pilot.
- Expand to Spanish-language opportunities across Latin America.

## Defensibility

- A consent-centered workflow designed for human-agent collaboration.
- Structured requirement data and reusable evidence mappings.
- Institution-level insight into where candidates abandon applications.
- Local-language and accessibility expertise for underserved applicants.

## Responsible growth

The product must never promise acceptance, infer protected characteristics, sell
candidate data, or hide sponsored ranking. Real documents require encryption,
short retention defaults, access logs, export, and deletion controls.
